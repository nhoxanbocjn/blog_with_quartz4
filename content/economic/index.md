## 📊 RSI (Relative Strength Index)
Range: 0 → 100 

Measures: speed & strength of price movement

Interpretation:

RSI > 70 → Overbought (price may drop)

RSI < 30 → Oversold (price may rise)

Formula (core idea):

RSI=100− (100/ 1+RS)
	​
👉 Where RS = average gain / average loss

## 📈 EMA (Exponential Moving Average)
A type of moving average that gives more weight to recent prices
Reacts faster than simple moving average (SMA)
Formula:

EMAt =α⋅Price t +(1−α)⋅EMAt−1
	​

👉 α (alpha) controls how fast it reacts
